<Release id="v2-1-0" version="Version 2.1.0" date="2026-10-25" label="25 October 2026" latest>

The biggest update since 2.0. Share your library, ask it questions, let terminal agents work with it, and export or import exactly the parts you want.

### Sharing

- **Share pages.** Share any mix of collections, bookmarks, notes and graphs with one link. Each share opens on this website, with each item in its own section and graphs shown as a board or a canvas. Formatting in notes and descriptions is kept; links, images and HTML in them are shown as plain text.
- **Unlisted, public or password protected.** A share is unlisted by default: only people with the link can open it. Make it public to list it on [Explore](/explore) and let search engines find it, or lock it with a password. A password-protected share and its comments are encrypted in your browser, so our server can’t read them.
- **Locked notes.** A password-locked note is only shared if you opt in, and then it stays encrypted. Locking a note that is already shared warns you and takes it out of the share.
- **Live shares.** Make a share live and it updates when the items in it change; other shares stay as they were when you made them. Open any item from Edit share to change it.
- **Comments.** Visitors can comment and reply, mark comments helpful and report them. New comments appear live. You choose whether comments are open, approved first or off.
- **Owner controls.** Revoke or delete a share, or set an expiry or a view limit. A 12-character code made on your device lets you manage your shares from any device. There is no account or email.
- **Forking.** Choose Save to Jupiter Jots on a share page, or Import a share in the extension and paste the link, to keep your own copy. A private share asks for its password in the extension, and locked notes stay locked.
- **Trends.** Each share shows views, forks and comments, with a daily trend for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Views and forks count once per visitor per day.

### Ask Jupiter

- **Ask your library.** A conversation inside the extension that finds saved items, summarises collections, suggests tags and prepares bookmarks, notes, collections, graphs and shares. Bring your own key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter; it stays encrypted on this device.
- **Review before it changes.** Jupiter batches what it wants to change into one proposal, with Apply, Discard and Undo. Nothing is saved until you apply it, and sharing or deleting always asks.
- **Longer tasks.** Jupiter plans multi-step work, shows what it is doing as it goes, and picks up an unfinished plan where it stopped. Errors show as a card you can retry.
- **Saved chats.** Keep a conversation, reopen it from the panel and delete it when you are done. Chats stay in this browser.
- **Models.** Pick a provider, model and effort under the message box. Refresh models fetches a provider’s latest list.

### Connected agents

- **Terminal agents.** A local MCP bridge, installed from npm as `jupiter-jots-mcp`, lets Claude Code and other terminal agents use the same library. Turn it on in Settings → Connected agents, then run `npx -y jupiter-jots-mcp` from your client. The connection stays on this computer.
- **Approvals.** Every write an agent asks for appears in Connected agents with the agent, tool and input, and a red pulse in the top bar tells you something is waiting. Turning the bridge off or revoking an agent denies what is waiting.
- **Tool permissions.** Settings → AI tools has an Assistant and an MCP column, each Off, Ask or On, plus read-only mode and per-group switches.

### Export & import

- **Export selected.** Pick individual items in each section of Import & export, or select items in the Library, Notes or Collections and choose Export selected.
- **Fuller imports.** Graphs, notes and saved chats in a backup are restored. Bookmarks you already have are referenced instead of duplicated, and you choose the destination collection. Every import can be undone.

### Library

- **List and detail side by side.** On wide screens, lists keep their place while the item you open shows beside them, and each item has its own address. Notes opened from a collection open in the same view.
- **Search everything.** Search finds notes, collections, graphs and shares as well as bookmarks, and looks through titles, descriptions, notes and tags.
- **Reading list.** Mark bookmarks to read without moving them out of their collections.
- **Fresh page details.** When you have a saved page open, Jupiter Jots fills in a missing icon, description or preview image, and offers a changed title instead of replacing yours.
- **Graphs.** Note cards on the canvas are compact, with a short title and excerpt. Graphs can be deleted from the Graphs list and go to Trash first.
- **Activity.** A full activity log shows what changed and whether you, Ask Jupiter or a connected agent changed it.

### Also

- The [privacy policy](/privacy) and [terms](/terms) cover shares, your code, comments and the one cookie share pages use.
- The [Ask Jupiter guide](/guide/ask-jupiter) and [terminal agents guide](/guide/agents) cover setup, privacy and troubleshooting.
- Notes you hide from AI, and password-locked notes, stay invisible to Ask Jupiter and to connected agents.

<Release id="v2-0-0" version="Version 2.0.0" date="2026-10-18" label="18 October 2026">

A rebuild from the ground up. Jupiter Jots is now one app across Chrome’s toolbar, side panel and a full-screen library.

### New

- **One app in three places.** The popup, the side panel and the full-screen library share the same library and the same controls.
- **Nested collections.** Put collections inside collections as deep as you need, each with its own icon, emoji or site logo, and colour.
- **Import into named collections.** Imports from Chrome, bookmarks HTML, CSV or a JSON backup go into a collection you name. Folders become collections inside it with their nesting kept, duplicates are skipped, and any import can be undone.
- **Page descriptions.** Saving a page keeps its own description and preview image, and search looks through them.
- **Custom site shortcuts.** Set up to nine keyboard shortcuts that each open a site you choose.
- **Tag filtering.** Filter the library by tags, together with collection, favourites, archive and date.
- **Insights.** See what you open, when, and which saves you never go back to. The activity log stays on your device and can be paused or cleared.
- **New logo.**

### Also

- Save with <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> <Kbd>S</Kbd> or from the right-click menu, for pages and links.
- Saving a page you already have tells you instead of creating a duplicate.
- Library health finds duplicate bookmarks and lets you merge them.
- Export everything, or one collection, as JSON, bookmarks HTML or CSV.
