<Release id="sharing" version="Sharing" date="2026-10-23" label="23 October 2026" latest>

Share any mix of collections, bookmarks and graphs with one link.

### New

- **Share pages.** Every share opens on this website, with each item in its own section and graphs shown as a board or a canvas. Bookmarks show their site icons and preview images, and collections their icon and description, as in the extension.
- **Notes in shares.** Share a note on its own or inside a collection, listed before its bookmarks. Notes, bookmark notes and collection descriptions show their formatting: bold, italic, code, lists and quotes. Links, images and HTML in them are shown as plain text.
- **Unlisted, public or password protected.** A share is unlisted by default: only people with the link can open it. Make it public to list it on [Explore](/explore) and let search engines find it, or lock it with a password. A password-protected share is encrypted in your browser, and so are its comments, so our server can’t read them.
- **Explore.** Browse and search the shares people chose to make public.
- **Comments.** Visitors can comment and reply, mark comments helpful and report them. New comments appear live.
- **Owner controls.** Revoke or delete a share, set an expiry or a view limit, and choose whether comments are open, approved first or off.
- **Your code.** A 12-character code made on your device lets you manage your shares from any device. There is no account or email.

### Also

- The [privacy policy](/privacy) and [terms](/terms) now cover shares, your code, comments and the one cookie share pages use. Their 24 October update explains unlisted, public and password-protected shares.

</Release>

<Release id="v2-0-0" version="Version 2.0.0" date="2026-10-18" label="18 October 2026">

A rebuild from the ground up. Jupiter Jots is now one app across Chrome’s toolbar, side panel and a full-screen library.

### New

- **One app in three places.** The popup, the side panel and the full-screen library share the same library and the same controls.
- **Nested collections.** Put collections inside collections as deep as you need, each with its own icon, emoji or site logo, and colour.
- **Import into named collections.** Imports from Chrome, bookmarks HTML, CSV or a JSON backup go into a collection you name. Folders become collections inside it with their nesting kept, duplicates are skipped, and any import can be undone.
- **Page descriptions.** Saving a page keeps its own description and preview image, and search looks through them.
- **Custom site shortcuts.** Set up to nine keyboard shortcuts that each open a site you choose.
- **Tag filtering.** Filter the library by tags, together with collection, favourites, archive and date.
- **Insights.** See what you open, when, and which saves you never go back to. The activity log stays on your device and can be paused or cleared.
- **New logo.**

### Also

- Save with <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> <Kbd>S</Kbd> or from the right-click menu, for pages and links.
- Saving a page you already have tells you instead of creating a duplicate.
- Library health finds duplicate bookmarks and lets you merge them.
- Export everything, or one collection, as JSON, bookmarks HTML or CSV.

</Release>
