<Section anchor="why" title="Why it exists">
<Prose>

Browser bookmarks are good at remembering an address and bad at remembering why it mattered. A year later you have a folder called “Read later” with four hundred titles in it, and no idea which one had the paragraph you needed.

Jupiter Jots started in 2024 as a small Chrome extension to fix that: save a page, tag it, write a line about why. Version 2 rebuilds it as one app that works in the toolbar popup, Chrome’s side panel and a full-screen library, with nested collections, page descriptions and a proper import from Chrome.

It is free, it needs no account, and your library stays on your computer. Only what you choose to share, and comments on it, are stored on our server.

</Prose>
</Section>

<Section anchor="principles" title="How we build it">
<Defs className="mt-0">
<Def strong wide term="Local first">Your bookmarks are stored in your browser on your device. We hold no copy of your library, and no analytics watch how you use it. Shares you make, and their comments, are stored on our server so their links work. Export is always available, so leaving is easy.</Def>
<Def strong wide term="Fast">Saving takes one click or one shortcut, and search answers as you type. Everything is local, so there is nothing to wait for.</Def>
<Def strong wide term="Calm">No streaks, badges or notifications asking for attention. Jupiter Jots stays out of the way until you need something you saved.</Def>
</Defs>
</Section>

<Section anchor="ask" title="Asking more of your library">
<Defs className="mt-0">
<Def strong wide term="Ask Jupiter">A conversation inside the extension that answers questions from what you saved and prepares changes, using your own AI key. Every change is shown for you to approve, and notes you hide or lock are invisible to it. [How Ask Jupiter works](/guide/ask-jupiter).</Def>
<Def strong wide term="Connected agents">A local bridge for Claude Code and other MCP clients. The connection stays on your computer, reads follow your permissions, and every write waits for your approval. [Agents in your terminal](/guide/agents).</Def>
</Defs>
</Section>
